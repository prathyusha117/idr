🎓 UK University Partnership

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is an industry-led training and deployment institute focused on digital, cyber and technology risk roles in regulated, high-consequence environments.

Through an established partnership with a leading UK university, IDR combines academic insight with real-world projects — bridging the gap between theoretical knowledge and the demands of live industry practice across financial services, government and critical infrastructure.

Logo Design Note

Colour: The palette of orange (#FF6A00), white and black was chosen deliberately — orange conveys urgency, visibility and energy appropriate to risk and security; white provides clarity and accessibility; and deep black grounds the identity in a technical, professional tone consistent with the cybersecurity and regulated-finance sectors IDR serves.

Symbolism: The icon uses a three-dimensional cube rendered inside a hexagonal frame. The cube suggests structure, layered complexity and resilience — core qualities of sound digital risk management. The hexagon references the interconnected, networked nature of digital infrastructure and echoes conventions from security and technology iconography.

Typography: The wordmark uses Syne — a geometric, high-weight display font with strong technical character — set in two weights. "IDR" appears at large scale for instant recognition, with "Institute of Digital Risk" set in tracked capitals beneath a fine rule for hierarchy and legibility at both desktop and favicon sizes.

Variants: Two variants are provided — icon-only (for favicon, app icon and mobile header use) and icon + full wordmark (for the main site header, documents and marketing materials). Both are delivered as SVG for infinite scalability.
